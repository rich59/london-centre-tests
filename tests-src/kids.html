English Entry Test – Ages 7–12

Hello! 🚌 Welcome to the English Entry Test!

Teacher: type the child's name, test the sound and the microphone, then press Start. The browser asks for the microphone once when the test starts – tap Allow; it then stays on until the end of the test. 43 questions, 46 points (Beehive Starter → Beehive 6), about 40–50 minutes, in 7 sections: Vocabulary (8) · Grammar & dialogues (12) · Listening & pronunciation (6) · Reading aloud (3) · Speaking (3 × 2 points) · Reading (7) · Writing (4 points, 30+ words). Questions get harder as you go – it is OK not to know everything! Teacher: if a child is clearly lost, you may stop and press Finish on the writing page after a short attempt. Tap 🔊 to hear a question again.

Teacher: before the child starts, press 🗣️ Test speech recognition and say a sentence – it listens for 5 seconds and shows what it heard (or what is wrong).

Works offline. Only the speech recognition in the Reading-aloud section needs the internet (Chrome/Edge send the audio to an online speech service). When the file is opened from the computer, Chrome/Edge may ask for microphone permission again for speech recognition – click Allow (or the microphone icon in the address bar). Without it the test still records the child's voice and the teacher gives a score. Nothing else is sent anywhere.

Placement bands

Bands follow the Oxford Primary Entry Test guidance (scaled to 46, with Beehive Starter added). The placement is then checked against the level profile: weak basics (Starter–B1 items < 60%) cap it at Beehive 1; Beehive 5–6 items < 50% cap it at Beehive 4; very strong hard items can raise it one level.
